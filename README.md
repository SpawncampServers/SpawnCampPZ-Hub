## PVP & Safety System

**Rule 1:** The game technically lets any PvP player hit someone, even with Safety on. However on our server that's against the rules. If their safety is on, leave them alone.

- **Safety ON (You see Green Axes icon on the left of the screen):** You are in PvE mode. You cannot damage other players, and other players should not damage you.
- **Safety OFF (Skull CrossBones Icon by player name):** You are opting into PvP. You can fight other players who also have Safety OFF.
- **Toggle Timer:** Switching safety on or off takes **60 seconds**, with a **60-second cooldown** before you can toggle again — plan ahead, as you cannot flip it mid-fight.
- **Griefing Clause:** Attempting to bypass the safety system (e.g., using vehicle collisions, fire traps, or pulling zombie hordes onto safe players) is strictly forbidden and treated as griefing.

## Community Rules

By joining our Discord or playing on our servers, you agree to the following:

- **Respect Everyone** — Treat all members, players, and staff with respect. Harassment, discrimination, hate speech, or real-life threats will result in an immediate ban.
- **Keep Discussions Organised** — Use the appropriate Discord channels for chat, bug reports, suggestions, media, and support.
- **No Unsolicited Advertising** — Do not advertise other Discord communities, game servers, products, or services without prior staff approval.
- **NSFW Content** — Permitted strictly in designated age-restricted channels.

## Gameplay Rules

**Play Your Way** — Trade, build, raid, fight, roleplay, help others, form factions, or survive alone. If it is possible through normal gameplay and is not listed under prohibited behavior, it is allowed.

**Prohibited Behaviour:**

- Cheating, scripting, or using third-party software
- Item duplication or exploiting engine bugs and glitches
- Map/terrain exploits used to gain an unfair advantage
- Bypassing PvE safety via indirect damage (cars, fire, horde dragging)
- Intentionally causing server lag or instability
- Ban evasion

Violations result in a **permanent ban** and a complete character wipe.
